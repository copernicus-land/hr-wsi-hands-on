# WSI webinar: Applications and hands-on training

Explore **Fractional Snow Cover (FSC)**, **SAR Wet Snow (SWS)** and **monthly water masks (WLRA)** from the Copernicus Land Monitoring Service using the Oetztal catchment, Austria, in 2019.

## Contents

- [hands_on_webinar.ipynb](hands_on_webinar.ipynb) — participant notebook.
- [oetztal_euhydro.gpkg](oetztal_euhydro.gpkg) — EU-Hydro-derived study-area boundary.

## Get started

1. Download this repository using **Code → Download ZIP** and extract it.
2. Sign in to [CDSE JupyterLab](https://jupyterhub.dataspace.copernicus.eu/) with your CDSE account.
3. Upload the notebook and GeoPackage into the **same folder under `mystorage`**.
4. Open the notebook and follow **section 1** to complete the setup.

**Then pause until the presenter starts the exercise.**

Dataset references, attribution and licence links are provided in **section 2 of the notebook**.
